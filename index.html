About Us

Making a Difference in Our Community

Key Club is an international, student-led organization that provides its members with opportunities to provide service, build character and develop leadership. At Century High School, we pride ourselves on inclusiveness, leadership, and caring.

  • Service Building

    We dedicate hundreds of hours each year to local charities, events, and school cleanups.

  • Leadership

    Members have the chance to run for office and lead large-scale community projects.
